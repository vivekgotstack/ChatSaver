## Why I made it

I wanted my knowledge to move naturally between my laptop and phone. Obsidian was already part of
that workflow, but accessing the same vault across devices required a paid Sync subscription.

That friction became the reason for ChatSaver: a focused, local-first knowledge workspace that
works offline, opens anywhere, and includes cross-device sync without placing a paywall between me
and my own notes.

ChatSaver is not a to-do list and it is not merely a chat archive. It is a quiet home for ideas,
research, questions, answers, drafts, and the useful fragments that would otherwise disappear
across devices.

## What makes ChatSaver special

- **Local first by design** — the vault remains fast and usable without a connection or account.
- **Sync without a paid add-on** — signed-in notes stay aligned across browsers, installed PWAs,
  and the Windows or macOS desktop app.
- **Real device awareness** — see active devices and revoke access from devices you no longer use.
- **Notes that fit the thought** — begin with a blank page or build editable question-and-answer
  blocks from useful conversations.
- **Selective ChatGPT import** — bring in only the conversations worth keeping and preserve their
  useful structure.
- **Live multi-device changes** — titles, questions, answers, ordering, and edits synchronize rather
  than becoming isolated local copies.
- **A library, not a folder maze** — search, sort, favorite, archive, paginate, and reopen knowledge
  quickly.
- **Portable ownership** — export Markdown, text, or polished PDFs and keep a complete vault backup.
- **Works everywhere** — use the responsive web app, install it as a mobile PWA, or download the
  compact desktop application.
- **Account recovery built in** — email verification, password reset, rotating sessions, and
  per-device access controls protect the synced vault.

## Why it works better for this workflow than Obsidian

| ChatSaver | The difference for me |
| --- | --- |
| Cross-device sync is part of the product | My laptop and phone can share a vault without a separate Sync subscription |
| Browser, PWA, and desktop access | I can open the same workspace without configuring plugins or a filesystem |
| Structured Q&A and selective AI-chat imports | Valuable answers become editable knowledge instead of remaining trapped in chat history |
| Device and session controls | I can see where the account is active and remove access remotely |
| Built-in PDF, Markdown, text, and vault exports | My notes remain portable and useful outside the app |
| Local-first operation | Losing connectivity does not take the working library away |

Obsidian is a broad and powerful tool. ChatSaver is deliberately more focused: it removes the
specific cost and setup friction that stood between my own knowledge and my own devices.
